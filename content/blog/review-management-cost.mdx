## How Much Does Review Management Software Cost in 2026?

Review management software costs between $0 and $399 per month in 2026, depending on the platform, feature set, and number of business locations. Enterprise-tier tools like Birdeye and Podium start at $249-$299 per month per location, while newer AI-native platforms like ReplyEngine offer free tiers and plans starting at $39 per month.

The market has shifted significantly since 2024. According to a 2025 Grand View Research report, the global online reputation management market reached $5.2 billion, driven by small businesses adopting automated review response tools. A 2025 BrightLocal Consumer Review Survey found that 88% of consumers trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations, making review management software a necessity rather than a luxury for local businesses.

For single-location contractors, home service businesses, and local shops, the cost question comes down to whether you need an all-in-one marketing suite or a focused review response tool. Platforms like Birdeye, Podium, and Reputation.com bundle review management with messaging, surveys, and CRM features, which drives their higher price points. Dedicated review response tools like ReplyEngine and NiceJob keep costs lower by focusing specifically on Google Business Profile review management and AI-powered response generation.

The most significant pricing factor is per-location fees. Multi-location businesses on Birdeye or Podium can easily spend $1,000+ per month across three or four Google Business Profile listings.

## How Much Does Birdeye Cost Per Month?

Birdeye costs between $299 and $399 per month per location in 2026, with most plans requiring an annual contract. The Starter plan at $299/month includes review monitoring and response tools, while the Growth plan at $349/month adds AI-generated responses and campaign automation. The Dominate plan at $399/month includes full reputation marketing and competitive benchmarking.

Birdeye does not publicly list pricing on its website. The figures above come from verified user reports on G2 and Capterra as of early 2026. All Birdeye plans require a 12-month contract commitment, and cancellation before the contract term incurs an early termination fee. Setup fees range from $0 to $500 depending on the plan and negotiation.

For a three-location business, Birdeye costs a minimum of $897 per month or $10,764 per year on the Starter plan. Birdeye integrates with over 150 platforms including Google Business Profile, Facebook, Yelp, Healthgrades, and Zillow, which partially justifies the premium pricing for businesses that need broad review site coverage. However, according to a 2025 Womply study, 63% of local business reviews originate on Google, meaning many small businesses pay for integrations they rarely use.

## How Much Does Podium Cost Per Month?

Podium costs between $249 and $399 per month per location in 2026, with pricing that scales based on features and contact volume. The Essentials plan starts at $249/month and includes review invitations, a unified inbox, and basic reporting. The Standard plan at $349/month adds AI-assisted responses and payment processing. The Professional plan at $399/month includes advanced automation and API access.

Like Birdeye, Podium requires annual contracts on most plans. Podium's core strength is its webchat-to-text conversion and unified messaging inbox, which makes it more of a customer communication platform than a pure review management tool. Podium integrates with Google Business Profile, Facebook, and over 200 CRM and POS systems including Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, and Housecall Pro.

The per-location pricing structure makes Podium expensive for multi-location businesses. A five-location home services company would pay at least $1,245 per month on the Essentials plan. Podium has also faced criticism on Reddit and G2 for aggressive upselling during the contract period and difficulty canceling. That said, Podium maintains a 4.2-star average on G2 across 1,500+ reviews, with users praising its texting features and review request automation.

## What Features Should You Look For?

The essential features for review management software in 2026 are AI-powered response generation, automated review monitoring, negative review recovery workflows, dispute detection for fake reviews, and multi-location dashboard support. Any platform you evaluate should cover at least four of these five capabilities.

Here is a feature checklist to evaluate any review management platform:

- **AI Response Generation** -- The tool should draft personalized, on-brand responses using modern AI models like Claude or GPT-4, not rigid templates. Generic template responses damage credibility. A 2024 Harvard Business Review study found that personalized review responses increased customer return rates by 12% compared to templated replies.
- **Dispute Detection** -- Fake and spam reviews cost businesses an average of $10,000 per year according to a 2025 Transparency Company report. Your platform should automatically flag suspicious reviews and provide evidence for Google dispute submissions.
- **Negative Review Recovery** -- Platforms should offer private outreach to dissatisfied customers via email or SMS before a negative review becomes permanent. Recovery workflows convert 15-30% of negative reviewers into repeat customers.
- **Analytics Dashboard** -- Track response rates, sentiment trends, average star ratings over time, and review velocity. Data-driven businesses outperform competitors on customer satisfaction metrics.
- **Multi-Location Support** -- If you operate more than one Google Business Profile listing, the platform must support centralized management without per-location fees that multiply your bill.
- **Google Business Profile Integration** -- Direct API integration with Google Business Profile is non-negotiable. The platform should pull reviews in real-time and post approved responses directly.
- **No Annual Lock-In** -- Monthly billing with the ability to cancel anytime protects your business from paying for software that does not deliver ROI within the first 90 days.

Birdeye and Podium check most of these boxes but carry significant cost overhead. ReplyEngine covers all seven points at a fraction of the price, specifically designed for contractors and local service businesses managing Google reviews.
